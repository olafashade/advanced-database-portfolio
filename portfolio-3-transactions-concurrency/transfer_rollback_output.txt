--------------------------------------------------------------------
 SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RAISE EXCEPTION aborted the procedure.
2. The EXCEPTION handler's ROLLBACK undid any partial work (nothing
   had been applied yet, but the rollback guarantees this even if
   the failure happened mid-way through the debits/credits).
3. The audit INSERT happened AFTER the rollback, inside a fresh
   transaction that was then committed - that is why the audit row
   survives even though the transfer itself was undone.
